Workspaces

How Tabstead works

  1. A workspace is a set of tabs in this window.
  2. Click a workspace to switch. The other workspaces' tabs are hidden, never closed.
  3. + New makes a workspace. Double-click a name to rename it, click its dot to change the colour.
  4. Right-click a tab and choose Move to workspace. A tab in a Firefox tab group takes its group along.
  5. Pinned tabs show in every workspace.
  6. Search finds a tab in any workspace and takes you to it.
  7. Tabstead keeps a copy of every workspace and brings back anything missing when Firefox starts. Backups keeps restore points, saves a file and imports Simple Tab Groups backups.
  8. Alt+Shift+W opens this sidebar. Alt+Shift+Page Down and Page Up go to the next and previous workspace.

      Backups

      A restore adds back the tabs that are missing. Nothing is closed and nothing is opened twice.
